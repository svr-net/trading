Market topology & geometry

Per half-life of the stocks' correlations: the minimum spanning tree's mean edge length (the total dimension-0 persistence of the correlation filtration; it contracts when stocks move together), the effective dimension of the correlation matrix (participation ratio over the number of stocks), and the Riemannian (affine-invariant) distance of the signal families' recent covariance from its long-run shape. These feed the futures hedge forecast.

Transaction costs (%)

Market standard: 0.10% per side (commission and spread); stamp duty 0 (CFDs, spread bets, AIM shares; 0.50% for UK shares bought directly). The daily book trades every day, so it needs cheap dealing (CFDs, spread bets): its cost per side, slippage included.
