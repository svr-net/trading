Daily Trades

Up to 10 day trades for the next open: the shares a walk-forward model, learnt from every share's daily prices and volumes, expects to rise most from the open to the close. Each is a buy limit at a learnt distance from the last close: bought at the open when it opens at or below the limit, during the day if it trades down to it, otherwise not traded. Sold at the take-profit, the stop-loss or the close (the levels learnt from each share's own moves on its typical days over the last 100).

Each trade's buy limit from the last close, its expected return from the open to the close, and its stop-loss and take-profit from the entry. One unit in every trade, an unfilled one stays in cash; the backtest's daily result is the average of the day's trades, every prediction made only from earlier days. The backtest is after Interactive Brokers' commissions (£5,000 or $5,000 a trade) and, in the UK, 0.5% stamp duty on every buy; the model learns its limits and levels net of them.
