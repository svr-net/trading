Intraday strategy

Each day's 100 most traded shares, on 5-minute bars: bought 5 to 40 minutes after the open (the delay learnt), up to 10 trades, each sold at its learnt stop-loss, take-profit or the close. One unit in every trade; no fees or costs. The trades depend on the day's opening moves, so they are chosen after the open, not listed the evening before.
