How it works

    
Every evening a model learnt from each share's daily prices and volumes ranks the most traded shares by how much they are likely to rise during the next day, and picks up to 10. For each it sets a buy price a little below the last close, a stop below and a target above, all learnt from that share's own typical daily moves. The bridge on your NAS places the orders before the open, sells what is left at the close and records the fills; Results compares them with what the model expected.

    
Costs: Interactive Brokers' fixed commissions (£5,000 a trade in the UK, 1,000 elsewhere in each market's currency), UK stamp duty (none on AIM shares) and the European transaction taxes. Prices are not published under the data licence.
